Yuzhou Cheng（程誉洲）

I am a second-year PhD student in the Dyson Robotics Lab at Imperial College London, under the supervision of Andrew Davison and Pedro Mediano. Previously, I received my BSc from CUHK-Shenzhen, supervised by Zhi-Quan Luo, and my MSc from UCL, supervised by Dimitrios Kanoulas.

How can robot vision lead to spatial intelligence? I explore this question through distributed inference frameworks and 3D representations.
